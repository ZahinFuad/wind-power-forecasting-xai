# Results Summary

This repository corresponds to a submitted BIM 2025 camera-ready manuscript on explainable wind power forecasting. The manuscript PDF is not included in this public repository while publication is pending.

## Model Comparison

| Model | MAE | RMSE | Notes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| TCN, 15 epochs | 0.1195 | 0.1595 | Strongest MAE among saved baseline results |
| LSTM | 0.1207 | 0.1567 | Strongest RMSE among saved baseline results |
| CNN-LSTM | 0.1212 | 0.1596 | Competitive hybrid baseline |
| GRU | 0.1293 | 0.1661 | Recurrent baseline |
| Transformer | 0.1754 | 0.2226 | Underperformed compared with recurrent and convolutional models |

## XAI Findings

The saved SHAP and LIME summaries indicate that recent wind speed, especially `windspeed_100m_t-0`, was the most influential feature for the TCN model. Recent wind speed at 10 meters and short-lag wind speed values at 100 meters were also highly ranked.
